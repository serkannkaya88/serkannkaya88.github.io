1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how "Ada Macerasi" (also shown as "Popfair") (the "App") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

2. Information Collected

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, language settings.
  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA): Used by Google AdMob to display advertisements, only with your permission.
  • Usage and Analytics Data: Anonymous data about how the App is used (for example, levels completed and features used), collected via Firebase Analytics.
  • Diagnostic and Crash Data: Anonymous crash reports and performance data, collected via Firebase Crashlytics, to help us fix problems.
  • Anonymous Account Identifier: An anonymous user ID is created via Firebase Authentication to enable optional social features. No email address, name, or password is required.
  • Optional Social Data: If you use the leaderboard or friend features, a nickname you choose and your friend code are stored on Firebase servers so these features can work. Gifts you send to friends (lives or coins) are also processed through Firebase.
  • Game Data: Level progress, coins, and game state are stored locally on your device.
  • Character Names: If you choose custom names for the hero and the sibling, these are stored only on your device and are not sent to our servers.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob - For displaying advertisements. https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore, Authentication) - For anonymous analytics, crash reporting, and optional social features such as the leaderboard and friends. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your game progress and chosen character names are stored locally on your device; deleting the App removes them. Anonymous social data (nickname, friend code, gifts) is stored on Google Firebase servers to provide the leaderboard and friend features. We do not collect your email address, real name, or contacts.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy: serkannkaya88@outlook.com

Bu Gizlilik Politikasi, "Ada Macerasi" (Popfair olarak da gorunur) uygulamasinin nasil bilgi topladigini, kullandigini ve korudugunu aciklar. Uygulamayi kullanarak bu politikayi kabul etmis olursunuz.

2. Toplanan Bilgiler

  • Cihaz Bilgileri: Cihaz tipi, isletim sistemi versiyonu, dil ayarlari.
  • Reklam Tanimlayicisi (IDFA): Google AdMob tarafindan, yalnizca izniniz ile reklam gostermek icin kullanilir.
  • Kullanim ve Analitik Verileri: Uygulamanin nasil kullanildigina dair anonim veriler (ornegin tamamlanan bolumler), Firebase Analytics ile toplanir.
  • Tani ve Cokme Verileri: Sorunlari duzeltmek icin anonim cokme raporlari ve performans verileri, Firebase Crashlytics ile toplanir.
  • Anonim Hesap Kimligi: Istege bagli sosyal ozellikler icin Firebase Authentication ile anonim bir kullanici kimligi olusturulur. E-posta, isim veya sifre gerekmez.
  • Istege Bagli Sosyal Veriler: Lider tablosu veya arkadas ozelliklerini kullanirsaniz, sectiginiz takma ad ve arkadas kodunuz bu ozelliklerin calismasi icin Firebase sunucularinda saklanir. Arkadaslariniza gonderdiginiz hediyeler (can veya coin) de Firebase uzerinden islenir.
  • Oyun Verileri: Bolum ilerlemesi, coin ve oyun durumu cihazinizda yerel olarak saklanir.
  • Karakter Isimleri: Kahraman ve kardes icin ozel isim secerseniz, bunlar yalnizca cihazinizda saklanir ve sunucularimiza gonderilmez.

3. Ucuncu Taraf Hizmetler

  • Google AdMob - Reklam goruntulemek icin. https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore, Authentication) - Anonim analitik, cokme raporlama ve istege bagli sosyal ozellikler (lider tablosu ve arkadaslar) icin. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Veri Saklama ve Guvenlik

Oyun ilerlemeniz ve sectiginiz karakter isimleri cihazinizda yerel olarak saklanir; uygulamayi silerseniz bunlar da silinir. Anonim sosyal veriler (takma ad, arkadas kodu, hediyeler) lider tablosu ve arkadas ozelliklerini saglamak icin Google Firebase sunucularinda saklanir. E-posta adresinizi, gercek adinizi veya rehberinizi toplamayiz.
