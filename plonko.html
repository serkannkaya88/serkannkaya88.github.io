Privacy Policy

Plonko (“the app”) is a block puzzle game. We respect your privacy. The app does not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, emails, or any personal information directly.

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data

Your best score is stored only on your device and is not sent to us. The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
